# Pub Slice — Smallest Leverage Loop (seed for a dedicated session)

*Seed artifact, authored in the 2026-07-24 party session (Indie's proposal,
ratified by the table). This is the starting point for a **separate** party
session focused solely on the slice. It is deliberately tiny. Everything not
named here is out of scope for the slice.*

*Why this exists: three of four voices independently attacked the ORIGINAL pub
slice (greet → buy a round → watch a relationship meter warm) for proving a
relationship meter, not the core fantasy. It contained no "through others." This
seed replaces it with the smallest loop that actually proves **leverage**.*

---

## The loop

**Three actors as 2D portraits** (no 3D, no greeting-ladder rendering, no power
graph):

- **You** — the offscreen hand.
- **Bram** — your would-be proxy.
- **Cato** — the target you never touch.

**One edge type:** standing-between-actors.

**One player action:** *buy Bram a round* → raises Bram's standing-with-you.

**One proxy behavior (the whole point):** when Bram's standing-with-you crosses a
threshold, Bram **actively spends his own standing to act against Cato on your
behalf** — a discrete, logged move (vouch / lean / block), **attributed to Bram,
never to you.** This is *active dispatch*, not passive social-proof. Bram needs a
tiny action-selection step ("Bram now backs you → Bram spends standing to lean on
Cato"). That's a function and a line of text, not art.

**Skin:** portraits + a posture/standing icon + one line of event text per beat.

## Win condition for the prototype

The player:
1. watches Cato's situation change,
2. can **trace it back** through Bram to their own round, and
3. sees that **their name appears nowhere in the causal chain the world can
   see.**

## The single question the session answers

> **Does moving Cato through Bram's arm feel like unseen leverage — or like a
> meter with extra steps?**

If yes: the core fantasy is proven in the cheapest possible skin, and *then* we
spend the animation/diegetic budget. If no: better to know now than after the
mountain.

## Explicitly out of scope for the slice

- 3D characters, greeting-ladder rendering, body-language tells.
- Power / PageRank graph (hand-set numbers only).
- Two-tier scaling, far-region headlines.
- Nested goals, automatic effect-signature linking.
- Information agents, memory prosthesis UI.

## Stretch goal (only if time)

Bram's warm exterior hides a hostile allegiance, so the leverage is *risky* — a
first taste of the reading game layered onto the leverage loop. This is flavor on
top of the proven loop, **not** part of the proof.
